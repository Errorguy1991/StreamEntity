What is this? The FUSE buffer is the 256 MB piece cache that keeps playback smooth: Active is video buffered ahead of where you are watching, Stale is video already played — together they are the filled part of the 256 MB. The hit rate is different: it is the share of piece requests served from this cache instead of being downloaded from peers again. It fills while you stream and empties itself when the stream stops — no manual reset is needed. The Reset button only zeroes this readout when nothing is streaming.

☁️ How Cloud Streaming Works

BitTorrent requires raw TCP and UDP socket networking to discover peer swarms. You can connect this app to:

  • Home PC via Secure Tunnel: Run cloudflared or ngrok on your PC to stream to your mobile from anywhere outside home.
  • Personal Cloud VPS: Host StreamEntity on any Linux VPS with open network ports (Oracle Cloud Free Tier, Hetzner, etc.).
